# Lens: a screen or anything a person looks at and uses

Sit in the seat of the person who will use it, with their job and their hurry, not yours.

1. Who opens this, coming from where, to do what? What do they already know when they arrive, and what do they not?
2. Walk it step by step as that person. At each step: what do they expect to see, what do they see, what do they do next? Where would they hesitate?
3. What do they see when there is nothing yet, when there is a great deal, when it is slow, when it fails, when they lack the permission?
4. What happens if they do the wrong thing: can they see it, can they undo it?
5. Which words on it are yours and not theirs? Which control is there but does nothing for them?
6. On a phone, with one hand; on a second visit; when they come back after a week: is it still obvious?
7. What do the screens next to this one do in the same situation? A person expects the same.

Write the expected outcomes as what that person will see and be able to do, in their words.
